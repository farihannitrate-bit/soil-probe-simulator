Soil probe simulator, revision A

Set the soil, push the probe in to a depth mark and press the button. The device runs the same steps as the revision A firmware: the 5×7 font and portrait screen, frequency → x → VWC → colour with the stored parameters, the 15 s result, the low-battery and probe-fault patterns, and the 3 s hold for the soil-type menu. Soil behaviour is illustrative.
