Soil Probe Twin

The probe's own printed parts, pushed into a hand-watered maize plot near Tamale on a modelled Harmattan day. The lights, screen and button run the revision A firmware logic; the soil dries in the sun and wets when you pour a bucket.

A pit is dug in front of the probe so you can see the fork and the soil it reads, darker where it is wetter. Mast-free and to scale: the probe is 633 mm long.

Modelled soil and weather, not measurements. Soil water uses a three-layer bucket model with illustrative field capacity, wilting point and drainage for each soil, and about 4.5 mm/day of crop water use in the Harmattan. The "UENR example" calibration is the synthetic worked example from docs/calibration.md, not a fitted curve. Firmware logic follows soil-probe.ino, sensors.cpp, ui.cpp and oled.cpp revision A: f_air 500 kHz, f_water 100 kHz, 8 gates averaged, k_temp = k_ec = 0, low battery 4.40 V, very low 4.10 V (blue and green drawn dim below it, brightness TO VERIFY), display 15 s, menu timeout 15 s. With the UENR example only the Standard slot is calibrated (VWC = −0.206 + 64.222 x, thresholds 17.8 / 20.9 / 25.5 %); Clay and Sand keep placeholders and show UNCAL.
